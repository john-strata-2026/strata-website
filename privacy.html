Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Strata Utilities ("we", "us"), an independent energy auditing business based in the UK. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email us at john@stratautilities.co.uk.

What we collect and why

Data-Only LOA requests. When you request an LOA link, we collect your company name and work email address. We use them to send you the Letter of Authority, answer your enquiry and follow up about our services. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Chat assistant. Messages you type into the chat assistant are sent to our server and to Anthropic, whose Claude AI writes the replies. The conversation is held in your browser only while the page is open, and we do not save chat transcripts. Please don't share personal or sensitive information in the chat. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering visitors' questions.

Emails you send us. If you email us, we keep the correspondence so we can reply and keep a record of our dealings with you.

Website analytics. We use Umami to count visits and see which pages are popular. Umami doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify individual visitors. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used.

Technical information. Like most websites, our servers briefly process your IP address and browser details to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse (for example, limiting how many chat messages can be sent in a short time).

Meter and billing data

If you go on to sign a Data-Only LOA, we will receive historical meter data from your network operator, and sometimes billing data, about your business premises. We use it only to carry out the audit and services you have asked for. The LOA itself sets out exactly what we are authorised to request, and it gives us no authority to change, extend or switch your supply contracts.

Cookies

We don't use cookies for advertising or tracking, so there's no cookie banner. The chat conversation is stored only in your browser's memory and is forgotten when you close the page.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the website and the business, who may only use it on our instructions:

  • Render: hosts our chat and form server
  • Anthropic: provides the AI behind the chat assistant
  • Resend: delivers LOA request emails to us
  • Umami: privacy-friendly website analytics
  • Google Fonts: supplies the fonts this site uses, which means your browser connects to Google
  • Our website hosting and email providers

We may also share information where the law requires it.

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States. Where information leaves the UK, we rely on the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to two years after our last contact with you, unless you become a client. Client records are kept for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as we need them for legal, tax and accounting purposes (usually six years). Technical logs are kept for a short period only.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it
  • ask us to transfer it to another organisation
  • opt out of marketing emails at any time

To use any of these rights, email john@stratautilities.co.uk. We'll respond within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
