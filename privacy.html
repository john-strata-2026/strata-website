Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Strata Utilities ("we", "us"), an independent energy auditing business based in the UK. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email us at john@stratautilities.co.uk.

What we collect and why

Free bill check and LOA requests. When you ask for a free bill check or a Letter of Authority, we collect your company name and work email address. We use them to reply to you, carry out the check you've asked for, send you the Letter of Authority if you go ahead, and follow up about our services. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Bills you send us. If you send us a copy of an energy bill, we use it only to assess whether an audit is worthwhile and, if you become a client, to carry out that audit. Bills for a sole trader or partnership may contain personal information such as a name and address; we treat it as described in this policy.

Emails you send us. If you email us, we keep the correspondence so we can reply and keep a record of our dealings with you.

Website analytics. We use Umami to count visits and see which pages are popular. Umami doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify individual visitors. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used.

Chat assistant. Messages you type into the chat assistant are sent to our server and to Anthropic, whose Claude AI writes the replies. We keep a copy of chat conversations (your messages and the assistant's replies) in our server logs so we can review them and improve the assistant's answers. We don't record your name, IP address or other details alongside them. Please don't share personal or sensitive information in the chat. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering visitors' questions and improving our service.

Technical information. Like most websites, our servers briefly process your IP address and browser details to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse (for example, limiting how many chat messages or form submissions can be sent in a short time).

Meter and billing data

If you go on to sign a Data-Only LOA, we will receive historical meter data from your network operator or supplier, and sometimes billing data, about your business premises. We use it only to carry out the audit and services you have asked for. The LOA itself sets out exactly what we are authorised to request, and it gives us no authority to change, extend or switch your supply contracts.

Cookies

We don't use cookies for advertising or tracking, so there's no cookie banner. The chat window keeps your conversation in your browser's memory only while the page is open, and nothing is saved in your browser afterwards.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the website and the business, who may only use it on our instructions:

  • Render: hosts our chat and form server
  • Anthropic: provides the AI behind the chat assistant
  • Resend: delivers enquiry emails to us
  • Umami: privacy-friendly website analytics
  • Google Fonts: supplies the fonts this site uses, which means your browser connects to Google
  • Our website hosting and email providers

We may also share information where the law requires it.

Some of these providers are based in, or store data in, the United States. Where information leaves the UK, we rely on the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details, including any bill you send us for a free check, for up to two years after our last contact with you, unless you become a client. Client records are kept for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as we need them for legal, tax and accounting purposes (usually six years). Chat conversations and technical logs are kept for no more than 30 days.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it
  • ask us to transfer it to another organisation
  • opt out of marketing emails at any time

To use any of these rights, email john@stratautilities.co.uk. We'll respond within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. For complaints about our service rather than your data, see our complaints procedure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
