Complaints procedure

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Our commitment

We want to know if something has gone wrong. A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about our service, our fees, or the way we've treated you, whether you're a client or not. We take every one seriously, deal with it promptly, and use what we learn to improve.

How to complain

Email john@stratautilities.co.uk with "Complaint" in the subject line, or write to us at [registered office address]. Please tell us what happened, when, and what you'd like us to do about it. If you'd prefer to talk it through, say so and we'll arrange a call.

What we'll do

  • Within 2 working days: acknowledge your complaint in writing, tell you who is handling it and how to reach them.
  • Within 10 working days: investigate and send you a full written response explaining what we found, what we're doing to put it right, and any remedy we're offering.
  • If we need longer, we'll tell you why and when you can expect our response. We'll never take more than 8 weeks in total.

Where we've made a mistake, we'll say so, apologise, correct it, and refund or reduce any fees affected.

If we can't resolve it

If you're not satisfied with our final response, or 8 weeks have passed since you first complained, you can take the matter further:

  • Micro and small businesses: [Once we are a member of an Ofgem-approved redress scheme for third-party intermediaries, you'll be able to refer your complaint to the scheme free of charge. Scheme name, contact details and eligibility criteria to go here.]
  • Complaints about your data: the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.
  • Complaints about your energy supplier (rather than about us): contact the supplier directly first; if unresolved after 8 weeks, the Energy Ombudsman. Citizens Advice also offers free support to small businesses on 0808 223 1133.

Records

We keep a record of every complaint and how it was resolved for at least three years, so we can spot patterns and show how we've handled things if asked.
