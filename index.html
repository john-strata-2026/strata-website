Solar export aggregation

Your solar export is worth more than you're being paid for it.

Standard Smart Export Guarantee rates are set for households. If you're a farm, estate or factory exporting serious volume, you're being paid a retail rate for a wholesale product. We pool export across the sites we look after so it can be sold like the wholesale product it is.

Questions we get asked

How is this different from an energy broker?

A broker is paid by the supplier when you sign a contract. We're paid by you, on a flat fee, and our letter of authority can't be used to switch you. We have no stake in which supplier you use.

What does it cost?

A flat monthly retainer from £[000], set by the number and complexity of your meters and agreed before we start. On historical refunds we recover, we charge a [00]% success fee. Nothing else.

What if you don't find anything?

We tell you. The free first-bill check exists so that you don't pay a retainer on a site where there's nothing to find.

Do I have to change supplier to use the export service?

No. Your import supply stays exactly where it is. The export contract is separate, and you sign it directly with the offtaker, not with us.

Who sees my data?

Only us. We hold your meter and billing data under UK GDPR, use it solely for your audit and reporting, and delete it when you leave. Our privacy policy sets out the detail.
